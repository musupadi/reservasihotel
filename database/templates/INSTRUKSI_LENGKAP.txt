# INSTRUKSI PENGISIAN DATA HOTEL

## File yang harus diisi (urutan penting!):
1. 1_hotels_template.csv - Data hotel utama
2. 2_room_types_template.csv - Tipe kamar dan harga
3. 3_hotel_rooms_template.csv - Nomor kamar fisik

---

## 1. HOTELS (Data Hotel)
Kolom yang HARUS diisi:
- id: Nomor unik hotel (gunakan 32 untuk Hotel Testing)
- name: Nama hotel
- city: Kota
- address: Alamat lengkap
- description: Deskripsi singkat

Kolom OPSIONAL:
- country: Default "Indonesia"
- owner_name, owner_phone, owner_email: Info pemilik
- rating: 0.00 - 5.00 (default 4.50)
- status: "active" atau "inactive"
- image_url: Link foto hotel (bisa pakai unsplash.com)

---

## 2. ROOM TYPES (Tipe Kamar & Harga)

### UNTUK HOTEL ROOM (Kamar Menginap):
```
room_category = hotel_room
pricing_type = per_night
price_per_person = [harga per orang per malam]
min_capacity = 1
max_capacity = 2-4
Kosongkan: hourly_rate, half_day_rate, full_day_rate, full_board_rate
```

Contoh:
- Standard Room: Rp 500,000/person/night, capacity 1-2
- Deluxe Room: Rp 750,000/person/night, capacity 1-2
- Suite Room: Rp 1,200,000/person/night, capacity 1-3

### UNTUK MEETING ROOM (Ruang Meeting):
```
room_category = meeting_room
pricing_type = half_day
price_per_person = 0
half_day_rate = [harga paket 4 jam + 1x coffee break]
full_day_rate = [harga paket 8 jam + 2x coffee break + 1x lunch]
full_board_rate = [harga paket 12 jam + 2x coffee break + 2x meals]
min_capacity = 10-50
max_capacity = 25-100
Kosongkan: hourly_rate
```

Contoh:
- Meeting Room Kecil (10-25 orang):
  * Half Day: Rp 1,500,000
  * Full Day: Rp 2,500,000
  * Full Board: Rp 3,500,000

- Meeting Room Sedang (20-50 orang):
  * Half Day: Rp 2,500,000
  * Full Day: Rp 4,000,000
  * Full Board: Rp 5,500,000

- Meeting Room Besar (50-100 orang):
  * Half Day: Rp 4,000,000
  * Full Day: Rp 6,500,000
  * Full Board: Rp 9,000,000

AMENITIES (fasilitas):
Tuliskan fasilitas yang include, contoh:
"AC, Projector, Whiteboard, WiFi, Sound System, Coffee Break, Meals"

---

## 3. HOTEL ROOMS (Nomor Kamar Fisik)

Isi nomor kamar yang tersedia di hotel.

TIPS PENAMAAN:
- Hotel rooms: 101, 102, 103... (lantai 1)
- Hotel rooms: 201, 202, 203... (lantai 2)
- Meeting rooms: M-ALPHA-1, M-BETA-1, M-GAMMA-1

Kolom yang diisi:
- id: Nomor unique (mulai dari 200)
- hotel_id: 32 (untuk Hotel Testing)
- room_type_id: Ambil dari file room_types (id 43, 44, 45, dst)
- room_number: Nomor kamar
- floor: Lantai berapa (1-10)
- is_blockchain_enabled: 1 (bisa booking online) atau 0 (walk-in only)
- status: AVAILABLE

Contoh mapping:
```
Standard Room (id=43):
- 20 kamar: 101-110 (lantai 1), 201-210 (lantai 2)

Deluxe Room (id=44):
- 10 kamar: 301-310 (lantai 3)

Suite Room (id=45):
- 5 kamar: 401-405 (lantai 4)

Meeting Room Alpha (id=46):
- 3 rooms: M-ALPHA-1, M-ALPHA-2, M-ALPHA-3 (lantai 5)

Meeting Room Beta (id=47):
- 2 rooms: M-BETA-1, M-BETA-2 (lantai 6)

Meeting Room Gamma (id=48):
- 1 room: M-GAMMA-1 (lantai 7)
```
